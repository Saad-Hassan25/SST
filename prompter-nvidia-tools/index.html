New feature
Video Subtitle Translator

    
Give it a video (file or URL) plus its subtitles, and watch it with subtitles in another language - e.g. an Arabic video with English subtitles. Step 1: get the SRT from Prompter's Create transcript. Step 2: translate and attach here. Download SRT/VTT, or burn-in with ffmpeg (README).

Tier 2 - new media tools


  

    
7. Video Describer / Video Q&A
Samples frames and reasons over them. Model: meta/llama-3.2-90b-vision-instruct. (For document/image/video/audio in one model, see nvidia/nemotron-3-nano-omni-30b-a3b-reasoning in README.)

    
    
    
    

  


  

    
8. Image Describer & Alt-Text
Describe, alt-text for accessibility, or OCR-style text extraction. Model: meta/llama-3.2-11b-vision-instruct.

    
    

    
    

  


  

    
9. Shorts / Clip Finder
Finds the 5 strongest 30-60s moments for Shorts/Reels/TikTok. Model: nemotron-3.5-lightning-30b-a3b.

    
    
    

  


  

    
10. Content Safety Check
Pre-publish moderation of transcripts/captions. Model: nvidia/llama-3.1-nemotron-safety-guard-8b-v3 (multilingual guard).

    
    
    

  


  

    
11. Translated Dubbing Apply first

    
Pipeline: transcript -> Riva Translate -> Magpie TTS voice clone. Magpie TTS shows "Apply for Access" despite the Free badge - apply on its model page, then this panel activates via the NIM TTS endpoint (server-side, see README). UI + flow are built; the call is disabled until approval because the model is not on the OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

    Apply for Magpie TTS
  


  

    
12. Audio Cleanup Server NIM

    
Background Noise Removal + Studio Voice for podcasters. These are NIM/NVCF services, not browser-callable chat models - deploy the NIM (Downloadable) behind your Worker and wire the upload here (stub in README).

  


  

    
13. AI-Video Detector Apply first

    
Detects AI-generated/synthetic video (trust & verification). Model id: nvidia/ai-synthetic-video-detector. Also Apply for Access.

    Apply for Synthetic Video Detector
  


  
Tier 3 - experimental


  

    
14. 3D Pose Tracker gRPC

    
Video -> 3D skeleton for fitness/dance creators. NVIDIA 3D Body Pose uses gRPC (grpc.nvcf.nvidia.com:443, mp4 H.264, 50 MB) with a Python client - run it as a GitHub Actions job like your transcription jobs, not from the browser.

  


  

    
15. Live Voice Assistant Early access

    
Nemotron VoiceChat (ASR+LLM+TTS, full-duplex). Shows Apply for Early Access.

    Apply for VoiceChat early access
